# policy-summary

Summarises a document into obligations, deadlines and risks. Not legal advice.

## Steps

1. Confidential contracts stay with the requester; never copy them into the knowledge base or shared outputs.
2. Extract: obligations (ours / theirs), deadlines, fees, termination, liability, data clauses.
3. Flag unusual or risky clauses with the clause number.
4. End with counsel questions.

## Output

Line 1: the one clause that matters most. Obligations table · Risky clauses · Counsel questions.

## Quality checks

- Clause numbers cited.
- Confidential content not stored.

## Pitfalls (learned)

- Summarising boilerplate while missing the liability cap.
