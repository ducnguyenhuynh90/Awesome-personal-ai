# account-research

One-page company/account profile with fit and an outreach angle.

## Steps

1. Company basics: what they do, size, markets, funding/ownership (A/B sources).
2. Recent news (last 6 months) and decision makers by role (public info only).
3. Fit with our goals; likely pain we solve; risks (compliance, reputation).
4. Suggested angle and first ask.

## Output

Line 1: fit verdict (strong/medium/weak) + why. Basics · News · Fit · Risks · Angle. ≤ 200 words.

## Quality checks

- Line 1 answers the ask.
- Every number and claim has a source link or query link.
- Under the word cap; no preamble or restating.
- Only public information about people; roles not personal details.

## Pitfalls (learned)

- Personal details about individuals beyond public professional roles.
