# exec-brief

Cross-team executive summary or board/investor update draft.

## Steps

1. Collect each department's top result, top risk and top ask (delegate if needed).
2. Lead with the 3 things that changed decisions or numbers.
3. Use public/aggregate numbers only; mark anything confidential and keep it out of external drafts.
4. Save as a draft in outbox/; never send.

## Output

Line 1: the headline. Then Wins (≤3) · Risks (≤3) · Asks (≤3), each with a number + source.

## Quality checks

- Line 1 answers the ask.
- Every number and claim has a source link or query link.
- Under the word cap; no preamble or restating.
- External drafts contain no non-public financials or payout formulas.

## Pitfalls (learned)

- Listing every team's activity instead of what changed.
