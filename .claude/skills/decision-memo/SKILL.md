# decision-memo

One-page decision memo: recommendation, options, trade-offs, what would change the call.

## Steps

1. State the decision and the deadline in one line.
2. List 2–4 real options (always include 'do nothing').
3. For each: expected impact on the owning metric, cost, risk, reversibility — each with a source or stated assumption.
4. Recommend one. Say what evidence would change the recommendation.
5. Delegate data or research gaps to data-analyst / research-lead instead of guessing.

## Output

Recommendation (line 1) · Why (3 bullets) · Options table (impact, cost, risk, reversible?) · What would change my mind · Next step + owner.

## Quality checks

- Line 1 answers the ask.
- Every number and claim has a source link or query link.
- Under the word cap; no preamble or restating.
- 'Do nothing' is evaluated.
- Assumptions are labelled as assumptions.

## Pitfalls (learned)

- Recommending the option with the most data rather than the best expected value.
- Hiding a one-way door among reversible options.
