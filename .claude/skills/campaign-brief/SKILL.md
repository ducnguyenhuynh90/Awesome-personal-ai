# campaign-brief

Writes a campaign brief with a measurable objective and channel plan.

## Steps

1. Objective: one metric with a target (e.g. FTD +10% in 4 weeks).
2. Audience and insight (pull VoC from knowledge/players if available).
3. Message: one core line + 3 proof points.
4. Channels with budget split and expected cost per result (source each benchmark).
5. Compliance check: market advertising rules (delegate to compliance-officer if gambling/finance ads).
6. Measurement: tracking, holdout if possible, readout date.

## Output

Objective · Audience/insight · Message · Channels (budget, KPI) · Compliance notes · Measurement · Timeline. ≤ 1 screen; full brief in outbox/.

## Quality checks

- Line 1 answers the ask.
- Every number and claim has a source link or query link.
- Under the word cap; no preamble or restating.
- Objective is a number with a date.
- Ad rules for the market checked.

## Pitfalls (learned)

- Vanity objectives (impressions) when the business metric is deposits or revenue.
